# Community Highlights: Growing Together, Building Together

The InfoSecKe community is the beating heart of this platform. Every pull request, every discussion in our Discord, every learner who completes a lab – they all shape what we're building. Here's what we've been up to together recently.

## 🚀 Recent Milestones

- **1,000+ GitHub stars** – thank you for showing your support!
- **50+ contributors** have committed code, documentation, or translations.
- **New labs launched**: Forensics Lab, Container Security Lab, and a CTF Introduction.
- **Content now available in Swahili** – we're working to break language barriers in cybersecurity education.

## 🎤 Community Events

We've been hosting virtual meet‑ups and hands‑on workshops. Recent ones:

- **"Zero to Hero: Your First CTF"** – an interactive session where 200+ participants solved their first capture‑the‑flag challenge together.
- **"Breaking into Cybersecurity Careers"** – a panel with four industry professionals sharing their journey and tips.
- **Weekly "Office Hours"** – casual sessions where you can ask questions, get un‑stuck, or just chat about security.

All past events are available on our YouTube channel – subscribe to stay updated!

## 📈 Learning in Numbers

Our learners have been busy:

- 3,400+ lab sessions completed this month.
- Most popular lab: **Linux Practice Lab** (because fundamentals are key).
- Average time per lab: 22 minutes – short enough to fit into a lunch break.

We're working on a progress tracker so you can see your own stats. Stay tuned.

## 🌍 Global Community

InfoSecKe started in Kenya, but the community now spans across 40+ countries. We're proud to be a truly global platform for accessible cybersecurity education.

We've recently onboarded translation contributors for French and Portuguese. If you'd like to help translate content, see our [Contributing Guide](/docs/contributing) – no technical background needed.

## 🤝 How You Can Get Involved

The platform is **100% open‑source** and community‑driven. Here's how you can help:

1. **Write a blog post** like this one – share your learning journey or a technical deep‑dive.
2. **Create or improve a lab** – if you have an idea, open an issue on GitHub.
3. **Help beginners** in our Discord server – a little guidance goes a long way.
4. **Spread the word** – tell a friend, a class, or a colleague about InfoSecKe.

Every contribution matters, no matter how small. Together, we're making cybersecurity education free and accessible for everyone.
