## Baseline Controls

- Enable full-disk encryption on laptops.
- Turn on multi-factor authentication for email and cloud accounts.
- Apply operating system and application updates regularly.
- Maintain offline backups for critical data.

## Verification

Review your settings monthly and after major device changes.
